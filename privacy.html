Privacy Policy for Jobs and Gigs

Welcome to Jobs and Gigs! We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a positive experience on our platform. This Privacy & Policy document outlines how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website and use our services.

Last Updated: May 13, 2026

1. Information We Collect

Personal Information: When you create an account or apply for jobs through Jobs and Gigs, we collect information such as:

  • Name and contact information (email, phone number)
  • Resume and CV
  • Work experience and education history
  • Skills and certifications
  • Location and preferred job types

Usage Data: We automatically collect information about your interactions with our platform, including:

  • Pages visited and time spent on each page
  • Jobs viewed and applications submitted
  • IP address and browser information
  • Device type and operating system

2. How We Use Your Information

Jobs and Gigs uses the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To facilitate your job applications and connect you with employers
  • To send you job alerts and updates relevant to your interests
  • To improve and personalize your experience on Jobs and Gigs
  • To communicate with you about account updates and important notices
  • To analyze usage trends and optimize our platform
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms of service
  • To prevent fraud and ensure security

3. Information Sharing and Disclosure

With Employers: When you apply for a job through Jobs and Gigs, your resume and profile information may be shared with the employer posting the job.

Third-Party Service Providers: We may share non-personally identifiable information with third-party service providers who assist us in operating our website and conducting our business, including analytics and cloud services.

Legal Compliance: We may disclose your information if required by law or if we believe in good faith that such disclosure is necessary to comply with legal obligations, enforce our policies, or protect the rights and safety of our users.

No Sale of Data: Jobs and Gigs does not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties.

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These measures include:

  • SSL encryption for data transmission
  • Secure authentication protocols
  • Regular security audits and updates
  • Restricted access to personal information

However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your personal information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Jobs and Gigs uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your browsing experience. Cookies help us:

  • Remember your preferences and login information
  • Track your activity and improve our services
  • Analyze user behavior and website performance
  • Personalize content and recommendations

You can control cookie settings through your browser, though disabling cookies may affect functionality on Jobs and Gigs.

6. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have certain rights regarding your personal information, including:

  • The right to access your personal information
  • The right to correct or update your information
  • The right to request deletion of your data
  • The right to opt-out of marketing communications
  • The right to data portability

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us through the information provided at the end of this policy.

7. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to provide our services and fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy. If you request deletion of your account, we will remove your data within a reasonable timeframe, except where retention is required by law.

8. Children's Privacy

Jobs and Gigs is not intended for individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18. If we become aware that a child has provided us with personal information, we will take steps to delete such information and terminate the child's account.

9. Third-Party Links

Our platform contains links to third-party websites and applications. When you click on these links, you leave Jobs and Gigs and enter another website. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of external websites. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party sites before providing your information.

10. Changes to This Policy

Jobs and Gigs reserves the right to update this Privacy & Policy at any time. We will notify you of significant changes by posting the updated policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date. Your continued use of Jobs and Gigs following the posting of revised policy means that you accept and agree to the changes.

11. Contact Information

If you have questions about this Privacy & Policy, concerns about how your information is being handled, or wish to exercise any of your privacy rights, please contact us:

  • Email: privacy@jobsandgigs.com
  • Mailing Address: Jobs and Gigs Inc., 123 Career Lane, Tech City, TC 12345
  • Contact Form: Contact Us Page

Your Trust Matters

We appreciate your trust in Jobs and Gigs. Your privacy is our priority, and we are committed to maintaining the highest standards of data protection and security.
